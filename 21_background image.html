Southeast University

Southeast University (SEU) is a well-known private university in Bangladesh. It was established in June 2002 with the goal of providing quality higher education at an affordable cost. The university is located in Tejgaon Industrial Area, Dhaka, and provides a modern academic environment for students from different parts of Bangladesh. Southeast University offers a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs through different departments and schools. Some of its major academic areas include Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), Textile Engineering, Architecture, Business Administration, Economics, English, Law, Pharmacy, and Information and Communication Technology (ICT). The university focuses not only on academic education but also on developing students' practical skills, creativity, communication abilities, leadership qualities, and problem-solving skills. Students have opportunities to participate in different clubs, seminars, workshops, competitions, cultural activities, and research projects. The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is one of the important departments of Southeast University. CSE students can learn subjects such as programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, computer networks, software engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, web development, and other areas of computer science. These subjects help students prepare for careers in software development, technology, research, and other IT-related fields. Southeast University also encourages research and innovation among students and faculty members. Through academic projects, research activities, and extracurricular programs, students can gain experience beyond the classroom. The university aims to prepare its students to face the challenges of the modern world and contribute to the development of society and the country.

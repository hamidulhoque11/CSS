Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a beautiful country in South Asia. It gained independence on March 26, 1971, after a liberation war. The capital city of Bangladesh is Dhaka. The official language is Bengali, and the currency is the Bangladeshi Taka. Bangladesh is famous for its natural beauty, green fields, rivers, and the world's largest mangrove forest, the Sundarbans. The country has a rich culture, traditional festivals, and delicious foods. The people of Bangladesh are friendly, hardworking, and welcoming. Agriculture, garments, and other industries play an important role in the country's economy. Bangladesh is a developing country with a growing technology and education sector. I am proud of my country, Bangladesh, and I hope it will become more prosperous and developed in the future.

Dhaka

Dhaka is the capital and largest city of Bangladesh. It is one of the most important cities in the country and serves as the center of government, business, education, and culture. Dhaka is situated beside the Buriganga River and has a rich history dating back several centuries. The city is famous for historical and cultural landmarks such as Lalbagh Fort, Ahsan Manzil, the National Parliament House, and Dhakeshwari Temple. Dhaka is also well known for its delicious food, busy streets, shopping areas, and colorful festivals. Although the city faces challenges such as traffic congestion, pollution, and overcrowding, it continues to grow rapidly and provides many opportunities for education, employment, and business. Overall, Dhaka is a vibrant and diverse city that represents the history, culture, and modern development of Bangladesh.
